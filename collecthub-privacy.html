This policy explains exactly what the app does with information. It is written to match

what the code actually does. If the two ever disagree, the code is a bug and we fix it.

The short version

  • Your collection stays on your phone. Items, shelves, photos, notes, prices and

tags are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. We never see them and

they are never uploaded anywhere.

  • There is no account. No sign-up, no email, no password, no profile.
  • Scanning a barcode sends one thing: the barcode number. Nothing that identifies

you or your phone goes with it.

  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects its own data. That part is

described below and is the only advertising-related collection in the app.

  • You can take your data out at any time — CSV export and a full backup file — and

delete everything from inside the app.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you create in CollectHub is stored locally, in the app's private storage:

  • shelves, items and the fields you define
  • photos you add to items
  • status (owned, wishlist, for trade, duplicate), prices, locations, notes, tags
  • your settings: language, theme, sort order, and whether you bought the unlock

None of this is transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

2. Barcode lookups — the one thing that leaves your phone

When you scan or type a barcode and the app looks it up, the app sends **the barcode

number only** to our lookup service at collecthub-lookup.seif-appslab.workers.dev,

hosted on Cloudflare.

  • What is sent: the barcode digits. Nothing else. No name, no device id, no

advertising id, no account (there is none), no list of what you own.

  • What our service does: it asks public product catalogues — UPCitemdb, Open

Products Facts and the eBay Browse API — for the product's name, brand and picture,

and returns that to your phone.

  • What we store: the pairing of *barcode number → product details*, so the next

person who scans the same barcode gets an instant answer. This is information about a

product, not about a person. We also keep daily counts of how many lookups succeeded

or failed, as plain numbers with nothing attached to them.

  • What we do not store: we do not keep logs of who looked up what, and we do not

build any profile. We have no way to connect a barcode to a person, because we never

receive anything that identifies one.

  • IP address: as with any request to any server on the internet, our hosting

provider (Cloudflare) sees the IP address the request came from, for the moment it

takes to answer it, and for its own network-security protection. We do not use it,

store it, or combine it with anything.

For books, the app queries Open Library and Google Books directly from the phone

instead, sending only the ISBN. For music, it may query MusicBrainz, sending only

the barcode. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Barcode lookup is optional in practice: if you add an item by hand without scanning,

no request is made, and the app works fully offline.

3. Photos and the camera

  • The camera is used for one thing: reading a barcode inside the scanner screen, and

taking a picture of an item if you choose to. Camera frames are processed on your

device and are never uploaded; the scanner does not save pictures at all.

  • Photos you attach to items come from your camera or from the system picker, and are

stored in the app's own storage on your phone.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of CollectHub shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google acts as an

independent controller for this and collects and shares the following, per Google's own

published disclosure:

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent screen

appears before the first ad and your choice is respected; you can change it later from

Settings → Ad privacy. Buying the one-time unlock removes all ads, and when ads are

off the advertising SDK is not asked for an ad at all.

5. Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we

never see your card, address or any payment detail. The app only receives Google's

answer that the purchase exists, and stores a flag on your phone.

6. Analytics

There is none. CollectHub contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no

tracking of how you use the app. The only usage numbers that exist anywhere are the

anonymous daily lookup counts described in section 2.

7. Children

CollectHub is not directed at children and is rated for a general audience. We do not

knowingly collect anything from children; in fact we do not knowingly collect anything

about any identifiable person.

8. Your control over your data

  • Export: Settings → Export, which writes a CSV file you keep.
  • Backup: Settings → Backup, which writes a zip containing your data and photos.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Erase everything removes all shelves, items, photos

and settings from the device. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.

  • Because we hold nothing that identifies you, there is nothing on our side for us to

delete, correct or hand over. If you believe otherwise, write to the address at the

top and we will answer.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and the updated

version appears at the same address before the change reaches the app.
