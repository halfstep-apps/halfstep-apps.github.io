Lawn Plan — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Lawn Plan: Lawn Care Schedule is made by Seif Aldeen Mahmoud Tawfiq Albataineh, trading as Halfstep. Contact: seif.appslab@gmail.com

This policy explains exactly what the app does with information. It is written to match what the code actually does. If the two ever disagree, the code is a bug and we fix it.

The short version

  • Everything you type stays on your phone. Your lawns, your log, nature checks and products are stored only in the app's own storage on your device.
  • There is no account. No sign-up, no email, no password, no profile.
  • The plan, the log, the tools and the guides work offline. They send nothing anywhere.
  • A banner ad is served by Google AdMob, which collects its own data. That part is described below and is the only data collection connected to the app.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you enter in Lawn Plan is stored locally, in the app's private storage:

  • your lawns: the name you give them, grass type, size, sun and your goal for the season
  • the country and rough climate you pick (no address, no ZIP code, no GPS location)
  • your log: what you did (mowing, fertilizer, weed preventer, weed killer, seed, water, aeration, dethatching, lime, notes), on which date, with the amounts, heights, product names, the day's high temperature and notes you type
  • your nature checks: the plant signs you tick and soil thermometer readings you type
  • the products on your shelf (name, N-P-K, bag weight, coverage, spreader setting), the jobs you skip, and your units (ft², lb, °F or m², kg, °C)

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

2. Notifications

The app can remind you when the next job on your lawn opens, on a date you planned instead, and to do the spring nature check. These reminders are scheduled and delivered by your phone's own system; nothing is sent to a server. On Android 13 and newer the app asks for permission first, and works fully without it.

3. Export (Pro)

With Pro you can export your log as a CSV file (the season report). The file is created on your phone and handed to Android's share sheet; it goes only where you choose to send it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the main screens, above the tab bar, supplied by Google AdMob. There are no full-screen ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this and collects and shares the following, per Google's own published disclosure:

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent screen appears before the first ad and your choice is respected; you can change it later from Settings → Privacy choices (ads).

5. Purchases

Lawn Plan Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, address or any payment detail. The app only receives Google's answer that the purchase exists and stores a flag on your phone. With it, no ad is requested at all.

6. Analytics

There is none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking of how you use it.

7. Children

Lawn Plan is not directed at children. Lawn products are for adults to handle. We do not knowingly collect anything from children; in fact we do not collect anything about any identifiable person.

8. Your control over your data

  • Edit or delete one entry: tap it on the season line, then Delete. Nature readings: Tools → Nature check → the × next to a reading.
  • Delete a lawn: Settings → the lawn → the bin icon removes it with its log (shown when you have more than one; for the last one, use Erase everything).
  • Erase everything: Settings → Erase everything deletes your lawns, the log, nature checks, your shelf and reminders. It does not remove Pro.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes all of it from the device.
  • Because we hold nothing that identifies you, there is nothing on our side to delete, correct or hand over. If you believe otherwise, write to the address at the top and we will answer.

9. Safety note

Lawn Plan gives estimates from general extension guidance. Always follow the label of the product you use, and ask a local extension office or lawn professional if something looks wrong.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and the updated version appears at the same address before the change reaches the app.
