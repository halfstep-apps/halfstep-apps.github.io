Splash Plan — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Splash Plan: Pool & Hot Tub is made by Seif Aldeen Mahmoud Tawfiq Albataineh, trading as Halfstep. Contact: seif.appslab@gmail.com

This policy explains exactly what the app does with information. It is written to match what the code actually does. If the two ever disagree, the code is a bug and we fix it.

The short version

  • Everything you type stays on your phone. Your pools and hot tubs, water tests, doses and products are stored only in the app's own storage on your device.
  • There is no account. No sign-up, no email, no password, no profile.
  • The plan, the log and the guides work offline. They send nothing anywhere.
  • A banner ad is served by Google AdMob, which collects its own data. That part is described below and is the only data collection connected to the app.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you enter in Splash Plan is stored locally, in the app's private storage:

  • your pools and hot tubs: the name you give them, size, sanitizer, surface, whether there is an ozonator, and the dates you mark for filter care and drain & refill
  • your water tests (chlorine, bromine, pH, alkalinity, calcium, stabilizer, salt, temperature) and when you took them
  • the doses you mark as added
  • the products you pick on your shelf and their strength, and your units (gallons or litres)

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

2. Notifications

The app can remind you to retest after a dose, to test again after a week, and to rinse the hot tub filter or change its water. These reminders are scheduled and delivered by your phone's own system; nothing is sent to a server. On Android 13 and newer the app asks for permission first, and works fully without it.

3. Export (Pro)

With Pro you can export your log as a CSV file. The file is created on your phone and handed to Android's share sheet; it goes only where you choose to send it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the main screens, supplied by Google AdMob. There are no full-screen ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this and collects and shares the following, per Google's own published disclosure:

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent screen appears before the first ad and your choice is respected; you can change it later from Settings → Privacy choices (ads).

5. Purchases

Splash Plan Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, address or any payment detail. The app only receives Google's answer that the purchase exists and stores a flag on your phone. With it, no ad is requested at all.

6. Analytics

There is none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking of how you use it.

7. Children

Splash Plan is not directed at children. Pool and hot tub chemicals are for adults to handle. We do not knowingly collect anything from children; in fact we do not collect anything about any identifiable person.

8. Your control over your data

  • Undo a dose: tap Undo under it on the plan.
  • Delete a pool or hot tub: Settings → the pool or tub → Delete removes it with its tests and doses (shown when you have more than one; for the last one, use Erase everything).
  • Erase everything: Settings → Erase everything deletes your pools and hot tubs, tests, doses, products and reminders. It does not remove Pro.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes all of it from the device.
  • Because we hold nothing that identifies you, there is nothing on our side to delete, correct or hand over. If you believe otherwise, write to the address at the top and we will answer.

9. Safety note

Splash Plan is a calculator. Always follow the label of the product you use, and ask a pool professional if something looks wrong.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and the updated version appears at the same address before the change reaches the app.
