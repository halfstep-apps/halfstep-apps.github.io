Food Handler — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Food Handler (a practice app for the food handler test) is made by Seif Aldeen Mahmoud Tawfiq Albataineh, trading as Halfstep. Contact: seif.appslab@gmail.com

This policy explains exactly what the app does with information. It is written to match what the code actually does. If the two ever disagree, the code is a bug and we fix it.

The short version

  • Everything you do stays on your phone. Your answers, practice exams, saved questions, language, state and exam date are stored only in the app's own storage on your device.
  • There is no account. No sign-up, no email, no password, no profile.
  • The questions, exams and guides work offline. They send nothing anywhere.
  • A banner ad is served by Google AdMob, which collects its own data. That part is described below and is the only data collection connected to the app.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you enter in the app is stored locally, in the app's private storage:

  • the language you study in (English or Spanish) and the state you pick
  • your exam date, if you set one
  • which practice questions you answered and whether you got them right
  • your practice exam scores, by topic
  • the questions you save, and whether you marked "I have my card"

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

2. Notifications

If you set an exam date, the app can send one short study reminder each evening until the day before the exam. These reminders are scheduled and delivered by your phone's own system; nothing is sent to a server. On Android 13 and newer the app asks for permission first, and works fully without it. Removing the exam date stops them.

3. Sharing and reporting

  • Share your result: the app makes a picture of your score on your phone and hands it to Android's share sheet; it goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Report a mistake / Request a feature: these open your own mail app with a draft filled in (the question number and its text). Nothing is sent unless you press Send there.
  • Official state page: where shown, this opens the state's website in your browser. That website has its own policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the main screens, supplied by Google AdMob. There is no ad while you answer questions or take a practice exam, and there are no full-screen ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this and collects and shares the following, per Google's own published disclosure:

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent screen appears before the first ad and your choice is respected; you can change it later from Settings → Privacy choices (ads).

5. Purchases

Food Handler Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, address or any payment detail. The app only receives Google's answer that the purchase exists and stores a flag on your phone. With it, no ad is requested at all.

6. Analytics

There is none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking of how you use it.

7. Children

The app is for people preparing to work with food, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect anything from children; in fact we do not collect anything about any identifiable person.

8. Your control over your data

  • Unsave a question: tap Saved under it again.
  • Remove the exam date: Settings → Remove exam date also stops the reminders.
  • Erase everything: Settings → Erase everything deletes your answers, practice exams, saved questions and the exam date. It does not remove Pro.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes all of it from the device.
  • Because we hold nothing that identifies you, there is nothing on our side to delete, correct or hand over. If you believe otherwise, write to the address at the top and we will answer.

9. Not an official exam

This is a practice app. It does not issue food handler cards and is not linked to any state, county, health department or course provider. The questions are written from the FDA Food Code 2022; your state or county may have its own rules.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and the updated version appears at the same address before the change reaches the app.
