Pantry Clock — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Pantry Clock is made by Seif Aldeen Mahmoud Tawfiq Albataineh, trading as Halfstep. Contact: seif.appslab@gmail.com

This policy explains exactly what the app does with information. It is written to match what the code actually does. If the two ever disagree, the code is a bug and we fix it.

The short version

  • Everything you add stays on your phone. Your items, dates, places, photos and shopping list are stored only in the app's own storage on your device.
  • There is no account. No sign-up, no email, no password, no profile.
  • Reading the date with the camera happens on your phone. The picture is never uploaded.
  • A banner ad is served by Google AdMob, which collects its own data. That part is described below and is the only data collection connected to the app.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored locally, in the app's private storage:

  • your items: name, kind (food, medicine, beauty, home), the printed date, quantity, the place you keep it, the day you opened it and how long the pack says after opening, and any note you type
  • whether you marked an item as used or thrown out, and when (this is what the stats count)
  • your places (fridge, freezer, pantry and any you add) and the photos you take of them
  • your shopping list
  • your settings: reminder days and time, haptics, celebrations, and how your packs write dates like 03/04 (day first or month first)

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it, photos included.

2. The camera and photos

The app asks for the camera permission so it can show a live view when you point it at a printed date. While the camera is open, the app reads the text in the live view a few times a second with Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs entirely on your phone. These frames are only held in memory while they are read, and are never saved or sent anywhere. When it finds a date (or when you tap the button), the app takes one picture. That picture is not sent anywhere either; it is kept in the app's private storage as the item's photo, and you can change or remove it.

When you choose a photo from your gallery instead, Android's own photo picker opens and the app receives only the one photo you pick.

A photo leaves the phone only inside a file you choose to share (a backup or your month recap), and then it goes only where you send it.

3. Notifications

The app can send one summary a day of what is coming up. These reminders are scheduled and delivered by your phone's own system; nothing is sent to a server. On Android 13 and newer the app asks for permission first, and works fully without it.

4. Backup, export and sharing

  • Backup: creates one file on your phone with your items, places, shopping list and photos, and hands it to Android's share sheet. It goes only where you choose (for example your own Google Drive or email). We never receive it.
  • Export (Pro): a CSV file of your items, handled the same way.
  • Shopping list and month recap: shared as text or a picture through Android's share sheet, only when you tap Share.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the main screens, above the tab bar, supplied by Google AdMob. There are no full-screen ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this and collects and shares the following, per Google's own published disclosure:

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent screen appears before the first ad and your choice is respected; you can change it later from Settings → Privacy choices (ads).

6. Purchases

Pantry Clock Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, address or any payment detail. The app only receives Google's answer that the purchase exists and stores a flag on your phone. With it, no ad is requested at all.

7. Analytics

There is none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking of how you use it.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect anything from children; in fact we do not collect anything about any identifiable person.

9. Your control over your data

  • Edit or delete one item: open it, tap the pencil, then Delete item.
  • Remove a place: Places → the place → the pencil → Remove this place (its items move to another place).
  • Delete all my data: Settings → Erase everything deletes your items, their photos, your places, the shopping list and reminders. It does not remove Pro.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it from the device.
  • Because we hold nothing that identifies you, there is nothing on our side to delete, correct or hand over. If you believe otherwise, write to the address at the top and we will answer.

10. About the dates

The app shows the date printed on the pack, or the one you enter, and counts down to it. It does not judge whether food, medicine or anything else is safe to use. Always check the pack and how it was stored.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it, and the updated version appears at the same address before the change reaches the app.
